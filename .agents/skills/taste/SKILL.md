# Design Taste & Art Direction Standards

This skill codifies the visual philosophy, aesthetic taste, and art direction rules that define the **Muthu** brand and its portfolio projects.

## Core Visual Philosophy

- **Cinematic & Editorial**: Every viewport must look and feel like high-end film key art or an avant-garde editorial spread.
- **Intentional Negative Space**: Whitespace (or dark negative space) is not empty; it gives gravitas, dignity, and visual focus to the key visual.
- **Restraint Over Clutter**: Resist adding decorative borders, unneeded badges, or redundant helper text. Let the imagery and typography command attention.

## Visual Hierarchy & Balance

### 1. Typography Pairings
- **Display Headings**: `font-horas` (all caps, high tracking, tight line-height, bold architectural presence).
- **Body & Secondary Copy**: `font-ppmori` (clean, humanist sans-serif with impeccable legibility, relaxed line-height).
- **Metadata & Technical Accents**: Monospaced font (`font-mono`), uppercase, tracking `[0.2em]` to `[0.3em]`, small size (`text-xs` / `text-[11px]`).

### 2. Palette Discipline
- **Primary Canvas**: Pure Black (`#000000`) and Deep Obsidian (`#0a0a0a`).
- **Signature Accent**: Warm Cinematic Gold (`#fec52d` with gradients transitioning to `#fce39d`).
- **Text Neutrals**: `#ffffff` for primary emphasis, `neutral-300` for secondary, and `neutral-500` for tertiary/metadata.
- Never introduce saturated, conflicting neon colors that fight with the artwork.

### 3. Edge-to-Edge Composition
- Key visuals, showcases, and masonry portfolios must embrace **full-bleed, zero-gap** architecture when requested.
- Borders between artwork tiles should be razor-thin (`border-neutral-900/60`) or completely seamless (`gap-0`).

### 4. Image Quality & Presentation
- All visual assets must be delivered in optimized WebP or AVIF formats.
- Images must maintain proper aspect ratios (`aspect-[1588/1985]` or similar) with `object-cover` and zero distortion.
- Always implement progressive loading and subtle dark hover sheens to create depth.
